Navidrome player for Android

Your albums,
without the gaps.

Coda plays music from your own Navidrome or OpenSubsonic server. Every screen takes its colour from the cover, and the music you play stays on your phone.

Get Coda for Android↓

Android 16 or later · phone
Your own Navidrome or OpenSubsonic server

Gapless

One album, one unbroken take.

Live albums, DJ mixes and concept records play exactly as they were cut. Coda joins tracks sample for sample, with no gap and no click, whether the next song is streamed or already on your phone.

  • FLAC
  • MP3
  • AAC
  • Opus
  • Ogg Vorbis

Colours from the cover

Every screen takes on the album.

Coda picks one accent from the artwork and uses it everywhere: the album page, Now Playing, the mini player and the buttons. Grey covers get a quiet silver instead of a forced colour.

Now Playing and the queue

Big artwork. Everything else within reach.

  • A waveform you can read. Each bar lights up as the song reaches it. Drag to seek.
  • Plain format info. A small chip says what is playing, such as “FLAC 912” or “MP3 270”.
  • A queue grouped by album. See what comes next, reorder it, or cache it ahead for a trip.
  • Pick up where you left off. Resume an album from any album list, and keep your queue across devices.

Music storage

Plays on, when the signal doesn’t.

  • An offline library that builds itself. The music you play stays on your phone, and Coda gives the space back when your phone needs it.
  • Prefer offline. Mark an album or playlist and Coda keeps it on the phone.
  • Offline playlists. A playlist shows how much of it is on the phone, like “8 of 14”, and skips what is missing.
  • You set the limit. Keep free space makes sure photos and apps always have room.

In the car

Android Auto and your voice.

  • Android Auto. Browse Home, albums, artists and playlists, and see the queue on the car’s screen. Offline, the car shows what is on your phone.
  • “Hey Google, play … on Coda.” Ask for music by name, in the car or on the phone.

Your server

Made for Navidrome and OpenSubsonic.

  • Original quality. Original files on Wi-Fi. On mobile data, lossless and high-bitrate files play as smaller Opus copies.
  • ReplayGain, if you want it. Album or track gain, picked automatically. Off by default.
  • Your plays count. Plays are sent to your server, also the ones you listened to offline.
  • Continue Listening. Home remembers your place in each album, like Coda for Mac.
  • One queue, many devices. Coda offers to take over a newer queue from another device. Nothing is overwritten silently.
  • Private by design. No analytics, ads or tracking. Coda only talks to your server.
